## Dated vs Undated: The 60-Second Version

**Dated planners** come with the calendar printed in. Every day already has a home, there's no setup, and a full year at a glance looks very satisfying. The catch: skip a few weeks and you're staring at a row of empty pages. It's also tricky if your year doesn't follow January to December.

**Undated planners** leave the dates blank, so you write them in as you go. You can start in any month, skip a week without guilt, and pick it up again later. The catch: a few minutes of setup each week, and no built-in holidays or reference calendar.

Here's a simple test. Think of the last planner you bought. If you used it faithfully until it ran out, a dated planner will suit you. If it went quiet by March, try undated.

## For the Sticker Lover: Pick for Space, Not Just Looks

If your planner is going to be covered in stickers (and ours are designed for exactly that), a few things matter more than the cover:

![Curvy Noodle planner stickers in a Neorah A6 planner with a Personal Saffiano Filofax, showing a colourful weekly spread](/images/blog/planner-stickers-neorah-a6-filofax-spread.jpg)

*Image: The Curvy Noodle Shop.*

- **Daily or weekly boxes of at least 2 cm wide.** Anything tighter and character stickers get cramped.
- **80 GSM paper or thicker.** It stops stickers wrinkling the page.
- **Lay-flat binding**, so the page stays open while you place stickers.
- **Some open space.** A planner printed wall to wall leaves nowhere for stickers to live.

An A5 weekly or a daily planner with a generous layout is usually the sweet spot. Our [2026 guide to planners that work with sticker sheets](/blog/the-best-planners-to-use-with-sticker-sheets-in-india-2026-guide/) goes deeper on sizes and paper, and the advice carries over to 2027.

## Before You Buy: A Quick Checklist

1. **Check the dates.** Confirm the planner starts in January 2027 and not as an academic year diary, and whether the week starts on Monday or Sunday.
2. **Check the paper.** Look for 80 GSM or above, and test your usual pen if you can. Gel pens and markers show through thin pages.
3. **Check the binding.** Hard covers and lay-flat bindings last longer in a bag.
4. **Check the size.** A5 is roomy but heavier. A6, B6 and Petite sizes travel better.
5. **Check stock early.** Popular colours and sizes sell out, especially undated ones.

Prices and availability above were checked in October 2026 and can change, especially launch pricing and limited edition sets, so confirm on the brand's own page before ordering.
