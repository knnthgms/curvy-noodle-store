Canterbury is a gorgeous old city, but if you're a stationery lover there's another reason to go: three of the cutest, most colourful shops you can find on a UK high street are all in the same place. **Flying Tiger Copenhagen**, **Typo** and **Smiggle** are all inside the Whitefriars Shopping Centre, which makes it very easy to build a stationery crawl in one afternoon.

I did exactly that, and came home with a pink pineapple, a tiny photo album, two little wallets and a sling bag. Here's a guide to each shop and what I picked up, with links so you can browse along.

## Quick Guide

| Shop | Good for | Where in Whitefriars | What I picked up |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Flying Tiger Copenhagen](https://www.whitefriars.co.uk/shopping/tiger/) | Quirky, low-price gifts, craft and desk bits | Unit 8, Whitefriars Square | Pink pineapple coin purse keyring |
| [Typo](https://www.whitefriars.co.uk/shopping/typo/) | Stationery, gifting, bags and tech accessories | 7 Gravel Walk | Mini Keychain Book, Mode Zip Wallet, Mode Zip Pocket |
| [Smiggle](https://www.whitefriars.co.uk/shopping/smiggle/) | Bright stationery, bags and lunchboxes | 21A Marlowe Arcade | Pink reversible sling bag |

All three are listed on the [Whitefriars Shopping Centre website](https://www.whitefriars.co.uk/), which is also where I found the addresses and opening hours above. Hours change, so it's worth checking before you go.

## Flying Tiger Copenhagen

**Best for:** small, happy, affordable things you didn't know you needed.

[Flying Tiger Copenhagen](https://flyingtiger.com/en-gb) is a Danish brand with shops all over the world, and the Canterbury branch is at Unit 8, Whitefriars Square (open Monday to Saturday 9am to 6pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm, according to the centre's website). Expect aisles of stationery, craft supplies, party bits, gadgets and quirky homeware, with price tags that make it very easy to say yes to one more thing.

**My pick: the pink pineapple.** It's a squishy silicone coin purse in the shape of a pineapple, with a bright pink crown, a zip down the front and a keyring so you can clip it to a bag. It was **£4** on the shelf when I was there. It's just the right size for hair ties, a lip balm, earbuds or a few coins, and I think it would make a very cute pouch for a handful of washi tape rolls.

I couldn't find this exact pineapple on Flying Tiger's website, so it may be an in-store or seasonal item. If you'd like something similar online, browse the [keychains collection](https://flyingtiger.com/en-gb/collections/keychains) or the [pink silicone flower purse](https://flyingtiger.com/products/pink-silicone-flower-purse-3068937), which has the same zip-and-clip idea.

## Typo

**Best for:** stationery, gifts and bags in colourful, slightly tongue-in-cheek designs.

Typo describes itself as your number one stationery and gifting destination, with novelty stationery, quirky home décor, kitchen goods and tech accessories. In Canterbury you'll find it at 7 Gravel Walk (open Monday to Saturday 9am to 5.30pm and Sunday 11am to 5pm, according to the centre's website). It's the one I'd send a planner person to first. I walked out with three things.

## Smiggle

**Best for:** bright stationery, bags, lunchboxes and gadgets.

Smiggle is another Australian brand, and the Canterbury shop at 21A Marlowe Arcade is small but very colourful (open Monday to Saturday 9am to 5.30pm and Sunday 10.30am to 4.30pm, according to the centre's website). It's aimed at school kids, but there's no rule that says grown-ups can't browse the pens, notebooks and bags.

Smiggle UK has other reversible bags in its [bags and accessories range](https://www.smiggle.co.uk/shop/en/smiggleuk/all-bags-accessories), for example the [Sawyer Reversible Shoulder Bag](https://www.smiggle.co.uk/shop/en/smiggleuk/bags-accessories/all-bags-accessories/sawyer-shoulder-bag-burgundy) and the [Tobi Reversible Messenger Bag](https://www.smiggle.co.uk/shop/en/smiggleuk/bags-accessories/all-bags-accessories/tobi-messenger-bag-black), and you can check what's in stock in Canterbury with [Smiggle's store locator](https://www.smiggle.co.uk/shop/en/smiggleuk/stores).

## Tips for Your Own Stationery Crawl

- **Do all three in one trip.** They're all inside Whitefriars, so you can fit them into one outing, with a coffee in between.
- **Check opening hours first.** Sunday hours are shorter in all three.
- **Look for the little things.** Keyrings, mini albums, pouches and wallets are the easy wins: small, cute, and they fit in a suitcase.
- **Stock changes.** My pineapple wasn't on Flying Tiger's website, and Typo and Smiggle ranges change with the seasons. If you see something you love, buy it.
- **Think about how you'll use it.** A zip pouch is a travel planner's best friend for washi tapes, stickers and pens.
