Can a passport size Traveler's notebook work as a planner? I'm finding out. I set up a tiny Traveler's Company passport notebook to plan **November and December 2026** as a two-month test for 2027, using five pages: a monthly overview, a content calendar, a to-do list dump, a photo pocket and habit trackers. The whole setup is in the video above, and this post walks through it step by step.

> **The short version:** I'm testing a passport size Traveler's notebook for two months before deciding whether to plan the whole of 2027 in it. The setup has five pages: **1) monthly overview, 2) content calendar, 3) to-do list dump, 4) photo pocket, 5) trackers**. Is it big enough? It's too early to say, which is the point of the test.

## What is a passport size Traveler's notebook?

A passport size Traveler's notebook is the compact size of the refillable notebooks made by Traveler's Company, the Japanese stationery brand. The refills measure roughly **124 × 89 mm** (4.8 × 3.5 inches) and the cover is slightly larger, around 13.5 × 9.8 cm, so it fits easily in a small bag or a jacket pocket. Measurements vary a little between shops, so check the listing if an exact fit matters.

## Is a passport size notebook too small for planning?

It depends on how much you write. Pages of about 124 × 89 mm suit compact layouts where each page has **one job** (a monthly overview, a list, a tracker) better than detailed daily schedules. That's exactly what I wanted to try, and why I'm giving it a two-month trial before committing it to 2027.

## What are the 5 pages in my 2027 test setup?

Before starting, I sketched the plan on a sticky note: five sections numbered 1 to 5. Here is what each one is and where to find it in the video.

### 1. Monthly overview (November and December)

The first section is a monthly overview covering November and December, the two months I'm testing. I set it up at [4:35 in the video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=275s).

### 2. Content calendar

A content calendar page helps you plan what to post and when. I draw the grid by hand ([8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=495s)), then add the dates and a notes column ([9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=560s)) so every date has room for notes.

### 3. To-do list dump

A to-do list dump is a page (or two) where every task goes, without worrying about order. I made these pages right after the trackers ([6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=405s)).

### 4. Photo pocket

The photo pocket holds favourite prints, so the notebook can carry a few pictures as well as plans ([10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=600s)).

### 5. Trackers

For habit tracking I used **mini sticky-note calendars**: one for a workout habit and one for doodling, labelled and placed on the November and December pages ([5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=330s)). They take up very little room, which matters in a notebook this small.

## How do you set up a passport size Traveler's notebook? Step by step

This is the order I followed in the video:

1. **Sketch your plan first.** Decide your sections before putting pen to paper ([1:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=70s)).
2. **Borrow inspiration.** I peeked at my weekly layout for ideas ([2:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=160s)). You can see that setup in my [plan with me weekly planner post](/blog/plan-with-me-my-weekly-planner-setup-using-curvy-noodle-stickers/).
3. **Paint soft yellow page borders** to frame the pages ([3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=195s)).
4. **Make the monthly overview** for November and December ([4:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=275s)).
5. **Add the sticky-note habit trackers** for workout and doodle ([5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=330s)).
6. **Set up the to-do list dump pages** ([6:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=405s)).
7. **Draw the content calendar grid**, then add dates and a notes column ([8:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=495s) and [9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=560s)).
8. **Add the photo pocket** with favourite prints ([10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=600s)).
9. **Do a final flip-through** to check everything is where you want it ([10:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zCY8Qw7isxk&t=630s)).
