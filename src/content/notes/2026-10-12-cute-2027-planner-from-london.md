I'm in Ramsgate, UK for about two months, spending time with my sister. In my very first week we headed to the town centre just to have a look around, and I found this absolutely cute petite planner by [Otter House](https://www.otterhouse.co.uk/). I may have squealed a little.

The cover is a black cat with a teal collar, sitting among orange gerberas, tiny daisies and a couple of butterflies, with a little "Cats 2027" label on top. It's a slim hardback, and the teal ribbon bookmark matches the cat's collar, which is exactly the kind of detail I fall for.

![A week-to-view spread for 27 September to 3 October next to a full-page illustration of a black cat leaning on a pumpkin by a white picket fence, with autumn leaves](/images/notes/otter-house-cats-2027-september-october-spread.jpg)

Inside, it's a week-to-view layout that starts on Monday, with each day on its own dotted line and the month written across the top in a typewriter-style font. Some weeks come with a full-page illustration on the opposite side. The autumn one here, with a black cat hugging a pumpkin, is my favourite so far.

![A week-to-view spread for 3 to 9 May next to a full-page illustration of a black and white cat sitting in front of a bookshelf of colourful books](/images/notes/otter-house-cats-2027-may-spread.jpg)

There's a tuxedo cat in front of a wall of colourful books for May too. Bookish cats are a very good sign.

![Two facing week-to-view pages for 18 to 24 January and 25 to 31 January, with tiny holiday notes and moon phase symbols](/images/notes/otter-house-cats-2027-january-spread.jpg)

It's practical as well as pretty. Holidays are printed in tiny text (UK bank holidays, Burns Night, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Australia Day and Mother's Day in several countries), and there are small moon phase symbols beside the days. The rows are slim, so I think it will be perfect for appointments, birthdays and quick notes rather than a full daily plan.

I plan to use it in my lineup next year, and I'll share more details about my setup soon.
