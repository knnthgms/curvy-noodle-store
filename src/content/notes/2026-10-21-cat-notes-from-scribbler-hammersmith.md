I had a day out in London recently, and with about five minutes to spare before we had to run for a train, I dashed into [Scribbler](https://www.scribbler.com/) in the Hammersmith Broadway Shopping Centre. Five minutes isn't much time in a shop full of cards and quirky stationery, but it was exactly enough to find these and know I couldn't leave without them.

They're the **What's the Cat Up To? Cat Notes** by [Pikkii](https://pikkii.com/collections/new/products/whats-the-cat-up-to-notes): a freestanding 200 page memo pad that comes in a teal box with a cat face on the front and little cat ear cut-outs on top.

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-box-back.jpg" alt="The back of the Cat Notes box, describing a freestanding 200 page memo pad with cat ears and 200 unique hand drawn illustrations, with a £7.99 price sticker" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

The back of the box says it all: it stands upright on your desk (the pad has its own cat ears!), and every one of the 200 sheets is a unique hand drawn illustration. The shop sticker says £7.99. The box also says it's printed with soy ink, which is a nice touch.

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-inside-box.jpg" alt="The inside of the box, showing the pad standing upright with black cat ears, four sample sheets and a sticky note reading Don't forget to buy cat food" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

Here's the fun part. Every sheet shows the same cosy living room, but the cheeky cat is somewhere different on each one, and the idea is to find out what he's up to before you flip the page over and write your note, because the back of every sheet is blank.

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-page-scratching.jpg" alt="A sheet from the pad showing a black and white line drawing of a living room with a cat scratching the wall beside a floor lamp" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-page-in-box.jpg" alt="Another sheet showing the same living room, with the cat peeking out of a cardboard box" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

On the pages I flipped through, he's scratching the wall by the lamp, peeking out of a cardboard box, sitting by the coffee table next to a mug, and (my favourite) leaving a bit of a mess behind him.

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-page-by-coffee-table.jpg" alt="A sheet showing the living room with the cat sitting beside the coffee table and a mug, and a scattered blanket on the floor" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

<img src="/images/notes/pikkii-cat-notes-single-sheet.jpg" alt="A hand holding a single sheet in front of the black cat ears of the pad, showing the living room with the cat perched up by the window curtains" style="max-height: 30rem; width: auto; max-width: 100%; margin-left: auto; margin-right: auto;" />

If you'd like your own, the [Pikkii website](https://pikkii.com/collections/new/products/whats-the-cat-up-to-notes) lists it at £8 (at the time of writing it showed as sold out, so I'm glad I grabbed mine on the way to the train).
